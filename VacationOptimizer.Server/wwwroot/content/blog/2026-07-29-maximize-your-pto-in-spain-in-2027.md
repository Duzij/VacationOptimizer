  In 2027, Spain's national public holidays fall on the following dates:

  | Date | Holiday | Day | Opportunity |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 January | New Year's Day | Friday | 3-day weekend |
  | 6 January | Epiphany (Three Kings' Day) | Wednesday | Midweek holiday |
  | 25 March | Maundy Thursday | Thursday | Holy Week bridge |
  | 26 March | Good Friday | Friday | Holy Week bridge |
  | 1 May | Labour Day | Saturday | Falls on the weekend |
  | 15 August | Assumption of the Virgin | Sunday | Falls on the weekend |
  | 12 October | National Day of Spain | Tuesday | Bridge opportunity (take Monday off) |
  | 1 November | All Saints' Day | Monday | 3-day weekend |
  | 6 December | Constitution Day | Monday | 3-day weekend |
  | 8 December | Immaculate Conception | Wednesday | Midweek holiday |
  | 25 December | Christmas Day | Saturday | Falls on the weekend |

  This is your sign to create a bridge around Epiphany on **6 January**, especially if you still need to visit family after Christmas and New Year. Simply take a couple of extra days off and enjoy an extended break.

  Another great opportunity is **12 October**. Take **Monday, 11 October** off and turn the public holiday into a relaxing four-day vacation.

  Similarly, you can play around with the **Immaculate Conception** holiday on **8 December**. Depending on your vacation allowance, taking a few additional days before or after the holiday can create another long and well-deserved break before Christmas.

  ## Frequently asked questions



  ### Is 12 October a national holiday everywhere?

  Yes — National Day of Spain (12 October) is a national public holiday, observed across the whole country. What varies by region are the additional regional and city holidays, which is why the planner supports the national calendar plus regional and city-level calendars for supported areas such as Barcelona and Madrid, giving you more bridge opportunities than the national dates alone。







  ### How do Barcelona and Madrid city holidays add options?



  In the [planner](https://longvacation.eu/app?country=ES&amp;year=2027), choose the city alongside the country and the calendar includes its local holidays — for example, Barcelona's city-specific days or Madrid's — on top of the national and regional ones. That can turn a midweek city holiday into another long weekend you would not see on the national calendar alone。 The post links above open the planner with each city preselected。





  ### What if a holiday falls on the weekend?

  Several 2027 Spanish holidays fall on weekends — Labour Day (1 May, Saturday), Assumption (15 August, Sunday)and Christmas (25 December, Saturday) — so they add no extra day off by themselves. Some autonomous communities move or compensate Sunday holidays with substitute days, so check your region's calendar; if a substitute applies, add it as a custom free day in the planner so your plan matches reality。
