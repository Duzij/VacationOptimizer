Why I built it

I built Vacation Optimizer because I kept doing the same calendar math by hand: checking public holidays, spotting long weekends, and trying to make a small vacation-day budget stretch further. The planner turns that repetitive counting into a clear set of options.

The goal is simple: help people see where their days off can create real rest, not just a longer spreadsheet of dates.

Who I had in mind

It is for employees, contractors, families, and managers who want a calmer way to compare plans before asking for leave or booking travel. I especially wanted it to help people who only get a limited number of paid days and need those choices to count.

What still needs judgment

The optimizer is a planning aid, not a final authority. Employer approval, local holiday verification, travel budgets, staffing needs, and personal commitments still matter before any final leave or booking decision.
