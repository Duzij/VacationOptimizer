What the site does

The public site explains the product, its limitations, and how to use the planner responsibly. The planner itself helps compare calendar options rather than leaving people to guess which days offer the best return from a fixed PTO budget.

Who it is for

Employees, contractors, families, and managers who want a clearer view of how weekends, public holidays, and PTO interact.

What it does not replace

Employer approval, local holiday verification, travel budgeting, staffing plans, and personal schedule constraints still need human review before any final leave or booking decision is made.
