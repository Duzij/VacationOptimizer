Vacation Optimizer

Vacation Optimizer is a free holiday optimizer and PTO optimizer that turns scattered public holidays, weekends, and regional holidays into longer, more useful breaks — currently covering India, Spain, and Indonesia with more countries on the way.

Pick your country, set a vacation-day budget with optional rules such as trip length or monthly caps, and get ranked time-off suggestions scored by days off gained per vacation day used. Plans stay in your browser and can be shared through a link.
