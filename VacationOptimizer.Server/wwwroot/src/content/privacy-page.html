Privacy policy at a glance

Last updated: 29 August 2026.

Vacation Optimizer is designed to work without optional tracking. We use only the browser storage needed for the planner by default. Optional product analytics and advertising technologies are disabled until you make an affirmative choice in the cookie banner.

Vacation Optimizer is the controller for the personal data described in this policy. To contact us about privacy, use the contact page.

This policy applies to the Vacation Optimizer website and planner. It does not replace the privacy information of third-party sites you choose to visit through external links.

PostHog product analytics

With your analytics consent, we use PostHog to understand how visitors use the site and improve the planner. Our PostHog endpoint is https://us.i.posthog.com, which is hosted in the United States.

We record limited product events, such as a page visit and planner actions (for example, creating or shuffling an optimisation, marking a calendar day, copying a share link, or sending feedback). Event properties are limited to planner settings and counts needed to understand the feature, such as country, year, number of vacation days, and action direction.

We configure PostHog with autocapture, session recording, error capture, and person profiles disabled. We do not intentionally send email addresses, feedback text, full URL query strings, planner seeds, or direct identifiers to PostHog. We remove query strings and referrer URLs before analytics events are sent.

PostHog may still receive technical information needed to operate the service, such as browser and device information, timestamps, and network information including an IP address at the point of connection. We do not use PostHog to identify people or include IP addresses as event properties.

Our lawful basis for this optional analytics is your consent. No PostHog events are captured until you opt in, and withdrawing consent stops future capture on this device.

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data stored on or accessed from your device. We separate necessary storage from optional analytics and advertising storage.

You can accept, reject, or select optional purposes in the cookie banner. You can reopen the settings from the footer or the control above at any time. Rejecting optional technologies does not restrict use of the planner.

Advertising and other service providers

If you opt in to advertising, we load Google AdSense. Google may process online identifiers, device and browser data, IP address, and advertising interaction data under its own terms and privacy policy. You can read Google's Privacy Policy.

We use Cloudflare, Inc. to protect and deliver the website. Cloudflare may process visitor traffic data such as IP address, browser details, device information, and security-related request metadata on our behalf. Its security controls may use strictly necessary cookies. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

If you use the contact or feedback form, the email address and message you submit are sent to Formspree so that we can receive and respond to your request. Do not include sensitive personal or employment information that is unnecessary for your query.

International transfers and retention

PostHog analytics data is sent to a United States endpoint. Transfers of personal data outside the EEA or UK may involve a country with different data-protection laws. We use the safeguards made available by our service providers, including data-processing terms and appropriate transfer mechanisms where required. You can review PostHog's GDPR information.

Planner data stays in your browser unless you share a link or submit a form. Optional analytics data is retained only for the period configured in our PostHog project and no longer than needed for product analysis. Feedback is retained only as long as needed to handle and follow up on the request, subject to legal obligations.

Country detection, tokens, and your choices

To suggest relevant vacation planning options, the server may read country headers supplied by infrastructure providers, such as CF-IPCountry, X-Country-Code, x-vercel-ip-country, and Fastly-Client-Country. The detected country code is used for the immediate response and is not stored in our database or linked to analytics by this feature.

When you generate alternative scenarios, the application uses signed tokens and planner seeds to reproduce results and avoid repeating them. They represent planner settings and output state, not your real-world identity. A share link can expose its planner settings to anyone you send it to, so share it carefully.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request or ask about this policy, use the contact page. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
