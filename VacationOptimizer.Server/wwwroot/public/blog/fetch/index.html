What the blog covers

The Vacation Optimizer blog publishes practical, country-aware vacation planning guides and product updates. Each post answers a real search question — such as how to maximize PTO in Spain, the Netherlands, the US, India, or Australia in 2027 — with exact public-holiday dates, bridge-day strategies, and planner deep-links.
