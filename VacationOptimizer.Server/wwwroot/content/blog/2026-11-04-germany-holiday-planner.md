  Choose any of the 16 federal states to combine the curated nationwide 2027 calendar with your regional public holidays, then let the optimizer place your PTO around those dates and weekends.

  ## A calendar for your state

  Bavaria's calendar includes Epiphany, Corpus Christi, Assumption Day, and All Saints' Day. Berlin includes International Women's Day. Brandenburg includes Easter Sunday and Reformation Day, while Thuringia includes World Children's Day. Other regional calendars cover their own observances, including Corpus Christi, Reformation Day, and All Saints' Day where applicable.

  Selecting a state also keeps your plan's scope clear. If you change regions, calendar choices tied to the previous holiday scope are reset rather than accidentally carried into an incompatible plan.
