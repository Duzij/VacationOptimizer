What users can expect

Informational planning support, interactive calendar suggestions, and a tool for testing vacation-day scenarios across supported countries and regions.

What remains the user's responsibility

Leave approvals, staffing coverage, compliance, travel purchases, and confirmation of final holiday accuracy remain outside the scope of the app.

Why this matters

The planner can highlight efficient date combinations, but a mathematically strong result is not the same thing as an approved or practical break. Users should always verify local reality before acting on a recommendation.

Service Limits and Fair Use

To ensure the service remains available and responsive for everyone, Vacation Optimizer enforces reasonable limits on usage. For example, the number of alternative vacation scenarios you can generate for a single set of settings is capped per session. If you reach this limit, you will need to adjust your parameters to explore different options.
