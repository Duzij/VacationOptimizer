  # Maximize Your Vacation in the US in 2027

  Americans, hear me out! Statistically, you are among the world's worst vacation-takers. Not because you don't enjoy vacations, but because many of you leave unused PTO days on the table.

  The good news is that the **US** and the **UK** are among the few countries that adjust the date of a public holiday if it falls on a weekend. For example, if a public holiday falls on a Saturday, it is usually moved to Friday. That's not common around the world.

  But even with this system, many Americans still don't use all of their paid vacation days. Let's change that. Capitalism can wait.

  What can you do to optimize your vacation in 2027? Below is the list of US federal holidays in 2027.

  | Date | Holiday | Day |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | January 1 | New Year's Day | Friday |
  | January 18 | Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. | Monday |
  | February 15 | Washington's Birthday (Presidents Day) | Monday |
  | May 31 | Memorial Day | Monday |
  | June 18 | Juneteenth National Independence Day (observed) | Friday |
  | July 5 | Independence Day (observed) | Monday |
  | September 6 | Labor Day | Monday |
  | October 11 | Columbus Day | Monday |
  | November 11 | Veterans Day | Thursday |
  | November 25 | Thanksgiving Day | Thursday |
  | December 24 | Christmas Day (observed) | Friday |

  To start with, you can create bridge holidays around the public holidays that fall on Thursdays. In 2027, these are **Veterans Day** and **Thanksgiving Day**. Simply take Friday off and, voila, you have a four-day mini vacation.

  To enjoy even longer vacations, combine your PTO with holidays that fall on Mondays or Fridays. This way, you can easily extend your breaks by adding just a few vacation days before or after the long weekend.

  Considering that the average American employee receives only **11 days of paid vacation per year**, it's definitely worth optimizing every single one. LongVacation.eu can help you make the most of them.
