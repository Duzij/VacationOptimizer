  If you're planning your annual leave, 2027's bank holidays are nicely placed for longer breaks and much needed rest.

  When a UK bank holiday falls on a weekend, a substitute weekday is normally observed. In 2027, Christmas Day and Boxing Day land on the weekend, so their substitute days are Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December! New Year's Day 2028 is observed on Monday 3 January, setting up a near-free year-end break. Nice!

  ## Best long-weekend windows in 2027

  - **Easter is the year's best deal.** Good Friday (26 March) and Easter Monday (29 March) already give you a four-day weekend for free! Take only three days between them — Tuesday 30 March, Wednesday 31 March and Thursday 1 April — and it becomes a 10-day break (26 March–4 April) for just three bookable days from your overall PTO budget!
  - **The May bank holidays are ready-made.** Early May (Monday 3 May) and Spring (Monday 31 May) each come with a free long weekend. Take Tuesday 4–Friday 7 May (four days) and the first one stretches from Saturday 1 to Sunday 9 May — nine days off.
  - **August needs a single day!** The Summer bank holiday falls on Monday 30 August, so one bookable day on Friday 27 August creates a four-day weekend.
  - **Christmas and New Year make a 10-day break.** Christmas Day (Saturday 25 December) is observed on Monday 27 December, and Boxing Day (Sunday 26 December) on Tuesday 28 December. Take Wednesday 29–Friday 31 December (three days) and you're off from Saturday 25 December 2027 to Monday 3 January 2028 — 10 days for three bookable days, with New Year's Day observed on the Monday. What an awesome way to reset and rest!

  ## Frequently asked questions

  ### What if a bank holiday falls on a weekend?

  A substitute weekday is normally observed. In 2027, Christmas Day (Saturday 25 December) is observed on Monday 27 December, and Boxing Day (Sunday 26 December) on Tuesday 28 December because Monday is already the Christmas substitute. Check your employer's holiday policy before booking time off.
