  A Canadian holiday calendar depends on where you work. Louis Riel Day belongs to Manitoba, Saint-Jean-Baptiste Day to Quebec, and Discovery Day to Yukon. A national list alone cannot show the full set of opportunities for a longer break.

  Canada has its own province- and territory-specific planner. Choose from all 13 provinces and territories to combine the curated nationwide calendar with your local 2027 holidays, then let the optimizer place your PTO around those dates and weekends.

  ## Local dates, one vacation plan

  The planner includes regional observances such as British Columbia Day, Manitoba's Louis Riel Day and Terry Fox Day, Quebec's Easter Monday and National Patriots' Day, and Yukon's National Indigenous Peoples Day, Discovery Day, and National Day for Truth and Reconciliation.

  Selecting a province or territory also keeps your plan's scope clear. If you change regions, calendar choices tied to the previous holiday scope are reset rather than accidentally carried into an incompatible plan.

  December 2027 is a bit special because 25th and 26th of December fall on a Saturday and Sunday, respectively. Both have a substitute day on December 27th and 28th!

  ## Try it

  Open the [Canada planner](/app?country=CA&provinceCode=CA-ON&year=2027&vacationDays=25), choose your province or territory, enter your vacation allowance, and run an optimization. Shuffle through the ranked alternatives to find the breaks that fit your year. Holiday dates and employer rules can change, so confirm them with official provincial or territorial guidance and your employer before booking.
