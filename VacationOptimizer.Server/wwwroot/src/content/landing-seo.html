Frequently asked questions

What does the optimizer do?

It builds a full-year calendar for your country and finds the best dates to take off around public holidays and weekends. You pick a country, year, and vacation-day budget; it returns ranked time-off ranges plus an annotated calendar. You can add office closures, ignore holidays that do not apply, cap days per month, and lock dates already planned.

How does bridge-day planning work?

A bridge day is a vacation day placed between a public holiday (or weekend) and another day off, connecting them into one longer break. If a holiday falls on a Thursday, taking the Friday off creates a four-day weekend with one PTO day. When holidays stack, three PTO days can become nine days off — as in our Netherlands 2027 guide. The planner scans the year, scores each bridge by days off per vacation day used, and fills your budget with the best options.

Is Vacation Optimizer free?

Yes. The planner, country showcase, and guides are free, with no account required. Everything runs in your browser.

Which countries are supported?

30+ countries, including the United States, Spain, the Netherlands, Australia, India, and Indonesia — with regional, state, and city holidays where they apply. City calendars such as Barcelona and Madrid are included.

Do you store my data?

No. Planner settings stay in your browser's local storage. There is no sign-up, and plans are shared only if you copy a link yourself.
