Optimization Logic

Greedy bridge-filling, not random date picking.

The service builds a full-year calendar, finds contiguous workday bridges between non-working clusters, scores them by days off gained per vacation day used, and applies the best options until the budget runs out.

Holiday Scope

Country, region, state, and city-aware inputs.

The frontend can call the general optimizer, or use country-specific optimize endpoints and schemas for India and Spain where holiday scope is more detailed.

Custom Overrides

Add office closures and ignore holidays that do not apply.

Requests support custom free days and ignored holiday dates, so the optimizer can account for company shutdowns, school schedules, or local exceptions.

Range Controls

Tune for short breaks or longer blocks.

Each request can constrain the minimum and maximum days per vacation range before a suggestion is accepted.

Planner Output

You get a full calendar plus ranked time-off ranges.

The API returns selected vacation days, grouped ranges, total days off, vacation days used, public holiday counts, and the full annotated calendar used to render the UI.

Saved State

Planner settings are normalized and shareable.

Requests are clamped, normalized, persisted locally, and can be represented in URL search params for returning to the same planning setup.
